六大设计原则 Six design principles

每一条都源于真实经营的痛 Each one born from real operating pain

原则 01Principle 01

AI-First

每个模块默认带AI，不是"可选附加"。AI是这个系统的神经，不是装饰。 Every module ships with AI by default — not as an "optional add-on." AI is the nervous system, not the decoration.

原则 02Principle 02

Observe,
Don't Type

系统主动从邮件、电话、IM、扫码中获取数据。零摩擦录入。 The system pulls data from emails, calls, IMs, scans. Zero-friction capture.

原则 03Principle 03

Suggest,
Don't Just Log

每条数据都触发"下一步建议"。AI是放大器不是替代者。 Every data point triggers a "next step." AI is an amplifier, not a replacement.

原则 04Principle 04

Voice-First

长按按钮说一段话，AI自动完成80%的常规操作。键盘是给员工用的，说话是给老板用的。 Hold the button, say a sentence, AI handles 80% of routine ops. Keyboards are for employees. Voice is for owners.

原则 05Principle 05

Hyperlocal

深度菲律宾化：BIR、SSS、Mayor's Permit、Padrino文化、Holy Week——国际产品永远做不到的层次。 Deep Philippines: BIR, SSS, Mayor's Permit, Padrino culture, Holy Week — depth international products will never match.

原则 06Principle 06

Human
in the Loop

关键决策仍由人批准。AI建议，人决定。这是信任的底线。 Critical decisions still need human approval. AI suggests, humans decide. That's the bottom line of trust.
